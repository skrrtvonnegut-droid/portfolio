# Patterns and templates

Patterns turn a successful piece of work into something reusable without pretending every environment is the same.

## Published technical patterns

- [Privileged Role Activation with Microsoft Entra PIM](../artifacts/identity-governance/privileged-role-activation.md) — time-bound administrative access with justification, validation, observability, and review.
- [Staged Windows Update Rollout](../artifacts/endpoint-management/staged-windows-update-rollout.md) — a deployment-ring model that constrains blast radius and makes exceptions and rollback explicit.

## Planned artifact families

- Governance registries and review cadences
- Operational health and risk digests
- Software evaluation and intake rubrics
- Design-document, runbook, procedure, and knowledge-article frameworks
- Incident and change learning records

A template should preserve the important questions and controls while leaving implementation-specific answers open.
