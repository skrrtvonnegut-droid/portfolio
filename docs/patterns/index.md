# Patterns and templates

Patterns turn a successful piece of work into something reusable without pretending every environment is the same. Templates preserve the important questions, controls, and evidence requirements while leaving implementation-specific answers open.

## Published technical patterns (3)

- [Microsoft 365 Tenant Health and Risk Digest](microsoft-365/tenant-health-digest.md) — turning identity, security, messaging, endpoint, licensing, integration, and automation signals into a concise risk-and-action briefing.
- [Privileged Role Activation with Microsoft Entra PIM](../artifacts/identity-governance/privileged-role-activation.md) — time-bound administrative access with justification, validation, observability, and review.
- [Staged Windows Update Rollout](../artifacts/endpoint-management/staged-windows-update-rollout.md) — a deployment-ring model that constrains blast radius and makes exceptions and rollback explicit.

## Published templates (2)

- [Incident and Change Postmortem Template](templates/incident-change-postmortem.md) — a blameless learning record covering impact, timeline, detection, containment, recovery, contributing conditions, corrective controls, ownership, and validation.
- [Software Evaluation and Intake Rubric](templates/software-evaluation-rubric.md) — a repeatable decision framework for assessing software value, identity, data, security, integration, support, cost, accessibility, and lifecycle ownership.

## Pattern families in development

- Governance registries and review cadences
- Design-document, runbook, procedure, and knowledge-article frameworks
- Identity and entitlement lifecycle controls
- Operational automation and exception-handling patterns
