# About

I am a Systems Administrator working primarily in the Microsoft ecosystem, with a focus on automation, identity governance, operational reliability, and technical knowledge that survives beyond the person who first solved the problem.

My work often sits between several layers at once:

- The immediate technical issue
- The control or dependency that allowed it to recur
- The people who need the system to remain usable
- The documentation and ownership model that keep it operable later

I am especially interested in systems that become quieter through better structure: clear ownership, explicit lifecycle states, reviewable automation, useful telemetry, and documentation that explains both **how** and **why**.

## Published operating model (1)

- [Making Tomorrow Quieter](operating-model.md) — the principles and operating loop I use to reduce recurring operational noise while preserving ownership, judgment, observability, and reversibility.

## Portfolio standards

Every substantive artifact identifies its provenance:

- **Original:** authored from my own analysis or implementation
- **AI-assisted original:** my work and reasoning, structured or edited with AI assistance
- **Adapted:** materially transformed from an attributed external source
- **External reference:** linked for context and never presented as my work

A polished document is not automatically evidence. The portfolio favors artifacts that reveal judgment, constraints, validation, and operational impact.
